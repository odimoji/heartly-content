Zara has the final run sheet for her ex's wedding.

Seven days.

She said yes, and she is running it.

Line fourteen. Planner to pin the groom's buttonhole.

Line twenty-two. Planner to cue the first dance.

Line thirty. Planner to hand the bride her bouquet, and step back.

She has done these jobs two hundred times.

Her hands know them.

Tonight her hands would not hold a pen.

She told Mrs Finch she would see it through.

That was yesterday.

Yesterday was braver.

She opens the app.

She writes: I have to stand a foot away from him and pin a flower on.

It says: the job puts you closer than the invitation did.

Then one question.

What are you afraid your face will do?

Swap the buttonhole to a colleague, or keep line fourteen.

What would you do?

Six days.
