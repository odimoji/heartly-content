Zara sent her best friend a forty item list about her ex.

Remy sent back one question.

Where is your half?

His wedding is in thirteen days.

She said yes, and until tonight it was entirely his fault.

Remy came round with wine and a pen.

Remy wrote the number one, and waited.

It took eleven minutes.

One. She stopped asking how his day was.

Somewhere in year two.

Two. She knew it was ending, and booked a holiday instead of saying so.

Three took longer.

Three was the car park.

She was the one who said, fine, go.

Remy did not say I told you so.

Remy topped up the glass.

That night she opens the app.

She writes: some of it was me.

It says: three items, and they cost more than his forty.

Then one question.

What changes if it was both of you?

Keep both lists, or delete his.

What would you do?

Twelve days.
