Zara has written her ex a four page letter.

She is not going to send it.

His wedding is in fifteen days.

She said yes.

Page one is the car park.

Page two is the hoodie.

Page three is mostly the word fine.

Page four surprised her.

It says: you never fought for us.

She read it back, on her sofa, at midnight.

Then she crossed out you, and wrote we.

Her best friend rang.

Zara read her page four.

Remy did not shout, for once.

Remy said, I wondered when you'd get there.

She opens the app.

She writes: I didn't fight for it either.

It says: the anger had a second name on it.

Then one question.

What happens to it now?

Burn the letter, or keep it in the drawer with the photo strip.

What would you do?

Fourteen days.
