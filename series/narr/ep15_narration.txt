Zara is alone in a meeting room at work, shouting at a chair.

The chair is standing in for her ex.

His wedding is in sixteen days.

She said yes. She is also planning it.

She fixed the seating plan last night.

His mother got her good table back.

Then the venue rang.

He has asked for the car park to be decorated.

They broke up in a car park.

So. The chair.

The chair had no answer.

The chair never did.

She said all of it.

The ticket machine. The rain.

The way he said take care.

A colleague opened the door, and closed it again.

That evening she opens the app.

She writes: I shouted at a chair and it helped.

It says: you finally said it out loud.

Then one question.

Who was it for?

Say it to him, or write it down and keep it.

What would you do?

Fifteen days.
