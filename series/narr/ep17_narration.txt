Zara is making a list of everything her ex did wrong.

She is on item thirty one.

His wedding is in fourteen days.

She said yes.

The letter is in the drawer.

The list is new.

The list is in a spreadsheet, because she is a professional.

Item four. Never once replaced the bin bag.

Item twelve. Said relax, during an argument.

Item nineteen. Liked his own photos.

By midnight there were forty items and a pie chart.

The pie chart was very satisfying.

For about a minute.

Then she noticed the list only had one name on it.

She opens the app.

She writes: it is all his fault and I have the data.

It says: forty items, and every one of them is his.

Then one question.

What is not on the list?

Send the list to her best friend, or add a second column.

What would you do?

Thirteen days.
