# Heartly's core story

Drafted 15 Nov 2026 from Odi's own positioning decisions (25–26 Oct and 14–15 Nov). Odi approves changes. Every script, caption and hook should come back to the one message below.

## Who it's for
Someone with a thing they can't stop thinking about. Usually a breakup or a situationship. Also a loss, something someone did to them, something they did, or a what-if that won't quiet down. They've tried the usual advice (keep busy, no contact, talk to friends) and the thought is still there at 2am. Most of the likes so far come from women in their late teens and twenties, so write for her first and for him second.

## The one message
You can't think your way out of the thing you can't stop thinking about. You get it out of your head by writing it down, and seeing what's underneath is what lets you put it down.

Short forms for hooks and captions:
- "for the thing you can't stop thinking about"
- "write it out. see what's underneath. put it down."

## What Heartly is, in one line
An iPhone app where you write out what's looping, it shows you what's underneath, and it walks you through it one conversation at a time.

## What it is not
Therapy, treatment, crisis support or medical advice. A breakup-only app. A place to vent in circles. A generic AI journal.

## Three things every piece can lean on
1. The loop is normal. Everyone has the private habit: the shower argument, the drafts, the profile check. Naming it is the hook.
2. The loop has a cause. Unanswered questions, an empty room at night, a feeling nobody named. Explaining it is the value.
3. Writing it out is the way through. This is the turn in every story, and the only place Heartly appears.

## Voice
A wry, warm older sibling. Honest and specific, a bit funny, never preachy. Lowercase on screen. It talks about real moments (11 drafts of one text, the voicemail you can't delete), never about "your healing journey".

## Honesty check (run on every hook, script and caption before it ships)
Heartly's audience is hurting, so content has to be compelling without leaning on that.
- No promised outcomes: never "get over them", "heal", "fix", "cure" or a timeframe. Say what the character did and what changed for them.
- No fear or shame as the lever: never "if you don't deal with this now", never "what's wrong with you". Validating and correcting are both fine; belittling is not.
- No fake urgency or scarcity, no fake reviews, testimonials or user counts.
- No invented numbers. Research only from scripts/FACTS.md, in its wording. A number that belongs to a character ("9 times today") must be shown as theirs, on their screen, never as a statistic.
- No clinical words (diagnosis, disorder, trauma response, anxiety as a condition) and nothing that reads as treatment.
- If a story touches on self-harm or a crisis, don't make it. That is not content for this account.
- The app is named once and described by what the character does with it. Since the paywall is coming, never say "free".
- Final pass: read it aloud. If it sounds written by a machine or a marketer, rewrite it (see the no-AI-tells list in PLAYBOOK.md).
