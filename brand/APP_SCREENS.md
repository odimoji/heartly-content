# What the real Heartly app looks like (from Odi's screen recordings, 16 Nov 2026)

Use this when drawing Heartly on a character's phone. The drawn screen must behave like the real app.

## Look
- Near-black warm background (#120d0a family) with a soft lighter glow top-right and fine grain. No cards, no chat bubbles for Heartly, no avatars, no bright colours.
- Heartly's words: plain cream text straight on the background, left-aligned, generous line spacing. Sans-serif in chats; Cormorant-style serif for onboarding questions and the "what I heard" summary.
- The user's words: small, right-aligned, in a faint dark rounded pill (stage chat). In onboarding the user's last answer shows as a small grey quote with a thin vertical bar on its left.
- Cream pill buttons, full width, dark text ("Go on", "Continue", "Yes, start with Blame"). Secondary button is a dark outlined pill.
- Small round cream send button with an up arrow; it turns into a spinner while Heartly thinks. Three small dots show while it is replying.

## Onboarding
- Header: back chevron top-left, "Heartly" in small serif centred, tiny progress dashes under it.
- One big serif question at a time ("When it's quiet and you're alone with yourself, what's the feeling that keeps coming back?"), placeholder "Start wherever you want", user types a short answer.
- Heartly reflects the answer in one or two sentences, then "WHEN YOU'RE READY" and the next question, with a "Go on" button.
- Ends on "WHAT I HEARD": a serif paragraph summarising the person in their own terms, a chip naming the module and "where we'd begin", the line "Does this feel right?" and two buttons: "Yes, start with <module>" and "Somewhere else feels closer".

## Stage chat (inside a module)
- Header: back chevron, small spaced capitals "UNDERSTANDING — STAGE 2", three-dot menu.
- Pattern of every Heartly turn: one short reflection of what the user just said, a blank line, then ONE question. The user replies in a few words. Heartly never gives advice lists or options here.
- Input: a dark "Reply..." field at the bottom with the round send arrow.

## What this means for stories
- Heartly reflects and asks; it does not hand out two fixes. The only real two-button moment is the end of onboarding.
- So a vote should be on what the character answers or does next after Heartly's question, not on "options Heartly gave".
