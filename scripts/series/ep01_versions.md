Each is 55 to 65 s, 26 to 28 shots, nothing held over 4 s. VO is Zara's own inner voice for now (the narrator question is settled at the voice step). Captions are 2 to 3 words in the lower third, keyword in capitals here = the coloured word. HUD sits top-left from the shot where it's first named.

Expression key: E1 neutral, E2 frozen smile, E3 dread, E4 caught out, E5 plotting, E6 hiding, E7 raw, E8 small real smile.

## Which one and why
**A.** Its first frame is the whole series in one second with the sound off: a thumb, a tick, "I said yes". It needs no reading beyond three words, it's the most common version of the feeling, and its last shot matches its first, so it loops. C has the best idea in it, which is why the order ticket is the day 13 turn in the outline: it lands harder once people know her. B's plus-one is days 20 to 22.

If you want the boldest opening over the clearest, pick C and I'll move the RSVP to day 2.
