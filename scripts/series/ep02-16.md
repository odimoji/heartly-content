# Episodes 2 to 16: scripts (overnight run, 17 Nov 2026)

Episodes 2 to 12 are built and rendered. Episodes 13 to 16 are built up to the voice: script, shots and stills exist, the narration is not recorded (ElevenLabs credits ran out). Odi approved episode 2 only and has not yet reviewed the rest.

Each line below is one shot: the words spoken while it is on screen. **Zara:** marks her own lines (ElevenLabs "Beth"); everything else is the narrator (ElevenLabs "Mike", one continuous take per episode). The vote and the path assumed are in the first line of each episode.

Checks run on each: opens on a different kind of moment and restates the premise (her ex's wedding, she said yes, days left) in the first lines; one line worth quoting; no vote counts; no promise the winner happens; honesty check against brand/MESSAGE.md (nothing promised, no shame, no research facts, no clinical words, the app is seen and never named aloud); no-AI-tells pass by hand. The repo's hook scorer was not used: it rates this narrator style low across the board, including the approved episodes.
