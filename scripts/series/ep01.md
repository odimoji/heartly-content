# Episode 1, approved script (Odi, 16 Nov): narrator tells it, Zara speaks twice

About 58 s, 24 shots, none over 4 s except the vote card. Every shot is full-bleed; a phone is always in her hand, in her room, with her lit face cut in between. N = narrator (neutral, dry, third person). Z = Zara's own voice, two lines only. Captions sit in the lower third, 2 to 3 words; the word in capitals and bold is the coloured one. The world is made up: her flat, the flower shop, the town. People: Zara, Miles (the ex), Ivy (the bride), Remy (best friend), Mrs Finch (her boss).

Heartly's screens follow brand/APP_SCREENS.md: tonight is her first time in the app, so it is the onboarding screen (serif question, her short answer, a one-line reflection, "WHEN YOU'RE READY", one question). Only two shots show the screen; her face does the rest. The vote card is ours, in Heartly's colours and type, and appears after Heartly's question: one wise option (CALL), one tempting (SEND). The episode does not promise that the winner happens. On Heartly's screens only the key phrase of each line is lit, so it reads in a second.

Expression key: E1 neutral, E2 frozen smile, E3 dread, E4 caught out, E5 plotting, E6 hiding, E7 raw, E8 small real smile.
