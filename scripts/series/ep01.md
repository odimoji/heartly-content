# Episode 1, approved script (Odi, 16 Nov): narrator tells it, Zara speaks twice

About 60 s, 24 shots, none over 4 s. N = narrator (neutral, dry, third person). Z = Zara's own voice, two lines only. Captions sit in the lower third, 2 to 3 words; the word in capitals and bold is the coloured one. The world is made up: her flat, the flower shop, the town. People: Zara, Miles (the ex), Ivy (the bride), Remy (best friend), Mrs Finch (her boss).

Heartly's screens follow brand/APP_SCREENS.md: tonight is her first time in the app, so it is the onboarding screen (serif question, her short answer, a one-line reflection, "WHEN YOU'RE READY", one question). The vote card is ours, in Heartly's colours and type, and appears after Heartly's question: one wise option (CALL), one tempting (SEND). The episode does not promise that the winner happens. On Heartly's screens only the key phrase of each line is lit, so it reads in a second.

Expression key: E1 neutral, E2 frozen smile, E3 dread, E4 caught out, E5 plotting, E6 hiding, E7 raw, E8 small real smile.

## Checks
- Send to a friend: yes, to the one who would also say yes out of pride.
- One line worth quoting: "She was not being mature."
- Works cold: shots 1 to 3 give the whole premise without sound.
- Cut already: a call from her mum, and "you can't undo it" (it needed explaining).
- Honesty: nothing promised, nobody shamed, the app is seen and never named aloud, no research facts.
- Spoken words: about 110, which is roughly 50 s at a calm pace, leaving room for pauses.
